About Me & Skills

"As an student in Mechatronics Engineering, I have interest spesificly in automation and electronics. Mechatronics is an exciting engineering field that combines three main disciplines: mechanical engineering, electronics, and programming. Instead of treating these areas separately, mechatronics brings them together to build smart, automated systems. Think of it as giving a machine a "body" (mechanical parts), a "nervous system" (electronic circuits and sensors), and a "brain" (software and microcontrollers). Everyday examples of mechatronics are all around us, from the automatic sliding doors at a grocery store and industrial robot arms to washing machines and smart home devices. By merging these technologies, mechatronics engineers can create efficient, precise, and automated solutions to solve complex problems in modern industries.
